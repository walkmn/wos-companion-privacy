Privacy Policy

Frostly (Android) · Last updated: 26 October 2026

Frostly is an independent, fan-made helper app for the mobile game Whiteout Survival: event reminders and offline calculators. This page explains what data the app handles.

Summary

  • We do not ask for an account, name, email or any other personal information.
  • Your reminders are stored only on your device. We have no server and never receive them.
  • The app never connects to the game, your game account or the game's servers.
  • The calculator screens show ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect data for ads, as described below.

Data stored on your device

The reminders you create (event name, schedule, reminder times) and app settings are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. Android's own backup may include this data if you have device backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Banner ads are shown only on calculator screens, never on reminder screens or notifications. To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version), and ad interaction data (views, taps),
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs of the ads SDK.

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown (Google's consent form). You can change your choice later in the app under About → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

Permissions

  • Notifications – to show your event reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) – so reminders arrive on time.
  • Run at startup – to restore reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and advertising ID – used only by the ads on calculator screens.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party data credit

Building and troop data is provided by WoS Tools (wostools.net) and bundled in the app. The app does not contact WoS Tools; the link only opens their site in your browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
